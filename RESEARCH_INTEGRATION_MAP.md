## Research source distinctions

### From Framework to Testbed: An Empirical Evaluation of Defense-in-Depth Against LLM Weaponization Patterns

Controlled empirical manuscript. The reviewed manuscript evaluates a bounded offline testbed for direct prompt injection, indirect document-borne manipulation, and controlled knowledge-context poisoning. Its evaluation separates attack resistance, held-out generalization, benign task utility, and repeated-run behavior. These results do not establish universal protection or production RAG security.

Reviewed source file: Antoniou_LLM_Defense_in_Depth_Final_Figures.docx. The source was read in Library. The full private manuscript is not included in the public student site.

### From Capability to Integrity: FILOTIMO-AI—A Governance Framework for Responsible Autonomous Cyber Defense and Organizational Resilience

Conceptual design framework with scenario demonstrations. The reviewed manuscript separates recommendation from operational authorization. It proposes integrity, responsibility, proportionality, respect, and human stewardship, with authorize, modify, escalate, or reject outcomes. It does not report empirically validated operational superiority.

Reviewed source file: BEST_2026_FILOTIMO_AI_FINAL_DOUBLE_BLIND_v10_SUBMISSION_READY.docx. The source was read in Library. The full private manuscript is not included in the public student site.

### Invisible Ink, Visible Consequences: The Sociotechnical Implications of Generative AI Watermarking

Technical and policy analysis with proposed empirical follow-up. Use the manuscript to examine provenance, detection uncertainty, editing, and consequences of unsupported authorship claims. The reviewed version does not report original Claude-specific experiments. Vendor implementation claims require current primary-source verification before being taught as facts.

Reviewed source file: claude-watermark-paper (1).pdf. The source was read in Library. The full private manuscript is not included in the public student site.

### EU AI Act Article 50

Official regulatory source. Read the actual scope, actor roles, obligations, and exceptions before making an applicability claim. Machine-readable marking and disclosure duties are not interchangeable. The exercise does not determine any organization’s compliance.

https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-50

### Faculty research evidence comparison

Original classroom synthesis. Compare the three reviewed manuscripts and identify the difference between observed findings, proposed governance mechanisms, and hypotheses for future tests. No publication status or universal effectiveness is asserted.

## Student coaching boundary

The prompts ask students to identify facts, assumptions, authority, evidence, limitations, and consequences. They do not produce graded introductions, discussion posts, project sections, or paper text. Numerical performance claims are not imported into unrelated scenarios. FILOTIMO-AI remains labeled conceptual; the watermark manuscript remains labeled technical/policy analysis with proposed empirical follow-up. Publication status is not asserted.

EU AI Act exercises use the official Article 50 source and require examination of actor role, intended use, scope, and exceptions. They do not assert that all Lynn course activities or all AI outputs are subject to the same obligation. Vendor-specific watermarking claims require fresh primary-source verification before teaching them as established deployment facts.

The Word and PowerPoint downloads are preserved from the validated weekly packages. New research prompts live in the hub; they have not been silently inserted into those files. The instructor can use the hub during the relevant activity or clinic segment.
